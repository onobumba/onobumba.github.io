## Content Posting Strategy

### Frequency
3-4 posts per week

### Best Posting Times
- **Tuesday-Thursday**: 9 AM - 11 AM (business hours)
- **Monday & Friday**: 2 PM - 4 PM (lighter business content)

### Content Mix
- **40%** Educational/Tips content
- **25%** Product/Service highlights  
- **20%** Company culture/behind-the-scenes
- **15%** Engagement/community building
