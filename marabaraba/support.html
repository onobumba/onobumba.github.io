Quick answers

How do I learn to play?

Open the book icon from the home or game screen to read the rules. Select Learn by Playing for the five-step interactive tutorial.

Does the game save automatically?

Yes. Your current match is saved on your device and can be resumed from Continue Game on the home screen.

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. Choose Pass & Play when starting a new game.

Does Marabaraba need an internet connection?

No. Computer opponents, pass-and-play, the tutorial, saves, and match history all work offline.

How do I control the sound?

Open Settings to switch music and sound effects on or off independently. Game audio may play while the iPhone Ring/Silent switch is set to silent when sound is enabled.

Are there subscriptions or in-app purchases?

No. Marabaraba is a one-time purchase with no advertising, subscriptions, or paid feature gates. Future updates are included.

Contact support

Email info@onobumba.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, Marabaraba version, and the steps that led to the problem. You do not need to send passwords or other private information.
