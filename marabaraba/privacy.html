Marabaraba

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 August 2026

The short version

Marabaraba does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or user accounts.

Information stored on your device

Marabaraba stores your current game, settings, and match history locally so the game can resume and remember your preferences. This information is not sent to Onobumba.

Deleting the app generally removes this local information, subject to the backup and restore behaviour configured on your device.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes your purchase through its store. Marabaraba and Onobumba do not receive your payment-card details. Each store's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Children

Marabaraba does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players.

Changes to this policy

If Marabaraba's privacy practices change, this page and the relevant store privacy declarations will be updated before the relevant app update is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@onobumba.com or visit Marabaraba Support.
